Strategy Discovery Console

A browser model of the strategy discovery layer in this repository, built from docs/design/strategy_discovery_design.md, its implementation plan and the twelve tutorials in tutorials/strategy-discovery/. One campaign is shown at every layer: the frozen policy, the candidate language, the identities, the interpreter, the counting of the search, the held-back data, the gate chain, the breeding search, the proposing agents, the shadow period, the audit from the ledger, and the execution boundary.
