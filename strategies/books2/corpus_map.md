# Corpus map: the highest-signal records per category

Date: 2026-10-06. Revision 1.

This is an appendix to `README.md`. It lists, for each of the twenty-eight categories, the thirty records with the highest census score in the 3095-record arXiv `q-fin.GN` harvest, so that the scan behind the briefs is traceable and the next reading session has a starting point. A record can appear under more than one category, because the census scores every record against every topic pattern.

The score is a keyword census over the record's title and abstract: eight points per topic pattern matched in the title, one point per further pattern matched in the abstract capped at six, two points for a journal reference, one for a 2022-or-later publication and one for a `q-fin` primary category. It is a relevance heuristic, not a quality judgement. A record marked with an asterisk is read in depth in the brief for that category; the rest are leads.
