# Corpus map: the highest-signal records per category

Date: 2026-10-05. Revision 1.

This is an appendix to `README.md`. It lists, for each of the sixteen categories, the thirty records
with the highest census score in the 2370-record `q-fin.TR` harvest, so that the scan behind the briefs
is traceable and the next reading session has a starting point. A record can appear under more than one
category, because the census scores every record against every topic pattern.

The score is a keyword census over the record's title and abstract: eight points per topic pattern
matched in the title, one point per match in the abstract capped at six, two points for a journal
reference, and one point each for a 2022-or-later publication and a `q-fin` primary category.
It is a relevance heuristic, not a quality judgement. A record marked with an asterisk is read in depth
in the brief for that category; the rest are leads.
